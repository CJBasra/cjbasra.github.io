Why take card payments?

When you can take payment there and then, you reduce delays, make it easier for customers to say yes, and get paid faster for the work, service, or sale you have completed.

Whether it is a deposit before an appointment, payment at the till, or the final balance after the job, card payments give customers flexibility without slowing your cash flow.

  • ✓Take deposits or booking fees upfront with Pay by Link
  • ✓Get paid as soon as the sale or job is done
  • ✓Give customers more ways to pay
  • ✓Improve cash flow with next-day settlement
