Online payments for independent businesses

Collect deposits, remote balances, and website payments without the chasing.

N1 Payments gives businesses the tools to collect deposits before appointments or projects begin, take remote payments during the customer journey, and get paid after the work or service is done without the customer needing to be in front of a terminal.

Remote links

Pay by Link

Send a secure payment link via WhatsApp, text, or email and let your customer pay in minutes. No card machine needed.

  • ✓Collecting deposits before projects, appointments, or bookings begin
  • ✓Taking payment remotely when the customer is not in front of you
  • ✓Following up on outstanding invoices
  • ✓Customers who prefer to pay from home at their convenience

Phone payments

Virtual Terminal

Take card payments over the phone through our secure virtual terminal. Log in via browser, enter the customer's card details with their consent, and get paid instantly.

  • ✓Log in via browser from wherever you are working
  • ✓Enter the customer's card details with their consent
  • ✓No terminal required, ideal for office-based admin staff taking inbound calls
