Merchant Cash Advance

Funding that grows with your card sales.

Access between £2,500 and £500,000 in working capital based on your monthly card turnover. Repay automatically through an agreed percentage of future card takings — no fixed monthly payments, no penalty for slower trading periods.

How it works for you

Flexible capital that moves with your business.

Speed

Funds available in as little as 24 hours.

A fast application process and upfront quote means capital can be in your account within one to a few business days of approval.

Flexible repayment

Pay back only as your customers pay you.

An agreed percentage of your daily or weekly card transactions is automatically collected — so on quieter days, you pay back less.

No collateral

Unsecured funding, no assets required.

A merchant cash advance is unsecured and based on your card turnover, not your assets. A fixed factor rate is agreed upfront — no hidden interest charges.

How it works

1

Apply in minutes

A straightforward application based on your monthly card turnover. No lengthy paperwork, no complex criteria — just a quick, clear process.

2

Receive a tailored quote

Your advance amount and fixed factor rate are agreed upfront. You'll know exactly what you owe before you accept — no surprises later.

3

Funds land fast

Once approved, funds are typically available within 24 hours to a few business days — ready to put to work immediately.

4

Repay through card sales

A small percentage (the holdback rate) is automatically collected from each card transaction until the advance is fully repaid. Busy month? Pay more. Quiet week? Pay less.

Eligibility criteria

A merchant cash advance is designed for businesses that take regular card payments. If you trade through a card terminal and have a consistent card turnover, you could be eligible in minutes.

✓

✓

✓

✓

Minimum of 10 card transactions per month

✓

Sole traders, partnerships, LLPs and limited companies welcome
